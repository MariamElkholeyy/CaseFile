Case: The Swapped Scarab · Recorded: 15 October 2026, 08:30

Three trays were in the preparation room yesterday: P, Q, and R. Their objects were packed before an afternoon move. The display label was printed before packing and has not been verified against the object now inside.

Your task: explain which preparation group belongs to the planned display, reconstruct its destination, establish who performed the wrong handover, and determine whether the later record was corrected honestly. Cite evidence and distinguish mistakes from deliberate actions.

You have complete copies of the relevant records listed in this case pack. Their statements may be mistaken, but the supplied system revision history and signed receiving observations are stipulated reliable within this fictional exercise. No unlisted secret passage, unknown suspect, or outside accomplice is required. The reproduction question and physical authenticity are separate: appearance alone cannot establish age.
