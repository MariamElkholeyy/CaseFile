# Visual asset provenance

Both raster assets were created using the built-in image generation tool in this task, and copied into this project. They are illustrative fiction, not evidence or documentary museum photographs.

## egypt-arrival.png
Prompt: Premium cinematic landscape for an Egyptian museum mystery website, 1536×1024. Giza dunes and pyramids, warm apricot and ivory late-afternoon light, generous sky and negative space, no text, interface or people.

## characters.png
Prompt: Four equal-width vertical panels in a horizontal 1536×1024 editorial portrait sheet. Invented contemporary Egyptian museum staff: Mariam Nabil, curator45 with dark shoulder-length curly hair and ivory/taupe clothes; Nadia Hassan, coordinator32 with low bun and rust/beige clothes; Omar Adel, receiving technician35 with short dark hair, beard and slate-blue shirt; Leila Farid, exhibition assistant28 with short wavy dark hair and cream blouse. Warm sandstone museum backdrop, natural neutral expressions, no villain cues, text or logos.

## 3D scarab
Original procedural geometry in scarab3d.js; stylized decorative sculpture, not an archaeological reconstruction. Three.js 0.170.0 bundled under vendor with MIT license. CSS selects individual portrait panels without modifying the original generated image.
